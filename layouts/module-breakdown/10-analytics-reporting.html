Module purpose

Analytics & Reporting

Aggregate operational and financial activity into dashboards that explain network performance, capacity, service and exceptions.

Upstream data→Analytics & Reporting→Downstream handoff

Inputs / Data

  • Order and trip histories
  • Status timestamps
  • Lane, terminal, weight and pallet measures
  • Revenue, carrier cost and margin
  • Audit and exception events
  • YTD CSV/JSON refresh artifacts

Core workflow

Load the embedded YTD analytics dashboard
Aggregate by time, terminal, lane and operating status
Calculate operational and financial KPIs
Display trends, scorecards and exception queues
Allow drill-through to the operational record

Key business logic

  • Operational states must share consistent definitions across modules
  • Analytics is a consumer of every module, not a terminal-only step
  • Current artifacts include live-week, terminal-performance and trip-control datasets
  • Source lineage is required for defensible KPI totals

Outputs / Next step

  • Executive and operational dashboards
  • Terminal/lane scorecards
  • Exports and prioritized action queues

KPIs

  • Open / Pre-Planned / Dispatched / In-Progress trips
  • Average capacity percent
  • Total linehaul revenue and revenue/trip
  • Gross profit and margin
  • Top lanes and terminal volumes
  • MABD urgent and other exceptions

Alerts / Exceptions

  • Stale data refresh
  • KPI source mismatch
  • Missing terminal/lane mapping
  • Revenue or capacity outlier
